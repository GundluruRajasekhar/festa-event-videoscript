Requirements

Festa is a GenAI-powered planning assistant for weddings, birthdays and community events.

Users

  • Families and individuals planning a personal celebration.
  • Community groups and organisers running local events.

Functional requirements

IDRequirement
F1User chooses an event type and enters guests, budget and date.
F2System splits the budget across categories for that event type and shows cost per guest.
F3System builds a dated timeline from today to the event day.
F4System lists the vendors needed and lets the user tick them off.
F5System suggests personalised ideas for themes, menu and activities.
F6User can revise inputs and regenerate the plan.

Non-functional requirements

  • Works on phones and desktops, with no sign-up needed.
  • Plan appears within one second of submitting.
  • Keyboard accessible, with readable contrast in light and dark mode.
  • Inputs stay in the browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Out of scope for the MVP

  • Online payments and vendor booking.
  • Live LLM calls; ideas come from built-in templates now and can move to an LLM later.
